4 · IN EVERY BUILD

No build types a price. It pulls one.

A build takes the entries it uses into prices.lock.json. konomify's basis gate — the gate every estate build passes — fails it if a locked entry differs from this registry, is missing from it, or is older than the registry allows, or if the build states a price anywhere that does not name a locked entry. FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do the prices come from?

Every price is a vendor's own list price or a public rate, quoted as the source states it, linked, and dated. The registry refuses an entry without a source, a date or a stated figure, and flags any entry older than 180 days.

Which subscriptions does the estate replace?

CRM (FallCRM Elite, the fallforce repository), the finance ledger (FallLedger) and HR (FallHR). Ticketing, e-signature ceremonies and payroll are not replaced: no estate organ covers them yet, and the page says so.

How finished is each replacement?

Each organ's rung on the ladder — Prototype, Works or Proven — is computed from GitHub's own record of what its workflows did, never typed. Proven means a mutation gate ran on GitHub's hardware and every mutant died; the page names the files that gate covers.

How do estate builds use the registry?

A build pulls the entries it uses into prices.lock.json. konomify's basis gate fails the build if a locked entry differs from the registry, is missing from it or is stale, or if the build states a price anywhere that does not name a locked entry.
